# Large Codebase Safety Rules

## Change Scope
- Changes must be minimal and localized.
- Do not refactor unrelated code.
- Do not "clean up" unless explicitly requested.

## Stability
- Existing behavior must not change unless required.
- Backward compatibility must be preserved.

## Predictability
- Similar problems must be solved in similar ways.
- Novel patterns require explicit justification.

Agents must optimize for long-term maintainability over short-term elegance.
